    How to play
    1. Each road has seven GAA questions. Play as many roads as you like.
    2. Each question has lots of right answers. Name one before the 30-second clock runs out.
    3. The fewer people who give your answer, the further down the road from your club it takes you. Wrong answers cost nothing, so keep guessing.
    4. Along the way you pass county grounds, the home clubs of legends and famous sights. Every road has different stops.
    5. Seven All-Star answers gets you all the way to Croke Park.
